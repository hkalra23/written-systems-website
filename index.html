The method

Two weeks, start to finish.

About twenty minutes of your time per process. Two short conversations from you. Everything else is our time.

Week 1

Session one

Pick the three jobs that hurt most and talk them through. No forms beforehand. About an hour of your time in total.

Week 1

Drafts back

Each procedure comes back in your words, with your exceptions. You mark what's wrong. The gaps that show up are the real problem.

Week 2

Build

A short follow-up call closes the gaps. One process becomes a working assistant. The log or form gets set up.

Week 2

Handover

Half an hour with the people who have to use it. Then it's theirs.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Can you just build the AI part?

I could, and it would work badly. An assistant built on an undefined process just automates the mess faster. The two weeks before it are what make it good.

How long does it take?

About twenty minutes of your time per process. Two weeks start to finish.

What if I hire someone instead?

You should. This makes the hire work. Day one with a written procedure is a different person from day one with "ask me when you're stuck".

Isn't this just SOP templates I could buy for $50?

You could, and they'd sit unfilled, like they have for everyone else who bought them. The blank page was the problem. You're paying for it to actually get done, in your words, in an afternoon.

My team won't use it.

That's the most common reason this fails. That's why the handover session is part of the work, not an add-on.

Who owns what you write?

You do. It's written in your words and it's yours. No platform you're locked into, no dependency on me.
